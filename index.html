How to play & photo privacy

Everyone submits or passes every category, then meets in the voting room. Vote once per contested category. You can vote for yourself once in the entire game, including a tie-break vote.

The photo with most category votes wins. A tie gets one final vote; another tie is settled by the first successfully received upload.

At the end, everyone taps Ready to finish. All uploaded photos, including the winner, and temporary game records are permanently deleted from the app’s backend. Photos you explicitly save to your phone remain. Unfinished games expire after 24 hours without activity; finished games expire after 15 minutes, checked every five minutes. Provider backups and browser or operating-system recovery are outside the app’s deletion controls.
