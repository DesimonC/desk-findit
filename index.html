Photo Safety Agreement

Only take and upload photos you own or have permission to share. Ask permission before photographing identifiable people, and obtain permission from a parent or guardian for children.

Do not upload sexual or nude images, abusive or hateful content, graphic violence, illegal content, or anyone’s private or sensitive information. Do not trespass or put yourself or others at risk to complete a challenge. You can always pass.

Your photos are shared with players in this game for voting. Use Report photo for a possible breach and explain your concern. The host can remove photos, warn players or block them from the game. Reports are sent privately to the host.

Game photos, including the winner, are scheduled for deletion when the host ends the game. Games also expire after 24 hours without a game action; automatic cleanup runs every 15 minutes and retries failed deletions. Background polling does not extend this period. Other players may capture or save images while they are visible; only share photos you are comfortable showing them.

Find It! Complete Rules

  1. Agree before playing. Read and accept the Photo Terms and Conditions on the opening page before hosting, joining or returning to a game.
  2. Host or join. The host chooses 1–10 random challenges from the 54 approved Classic challenges. New players join with a game code and name before play starts. Keep your private rejoin link safe to return to your player session, including during voting.
  3. Take your photos. Complete challenges in any order. Submit one photo per challenge or choose Pass. Follow the Photo Safety Agreement, respect privacy and never take risks for a photo.
  4. Host safety review. Once everyone has submitted or passed, the host selects Go to Voting to preview all submitted photos. Each photo has a Reject photo button. Rejected photos are excluded from voting and are not shared with other players. After reviewing all remaining photos, the host confirms at the bottom and selects Start Voting. Everyone, including the host, then votes; if no categories need a vote, category results follow instead.
    5. \n
  5. Vote anonymously. Each player votes once in every category with at least two submitted photos. A sole entry wins automatically; a category with no photos has no winner. The connected server reports an older self-vote rule: one self-vote per game.You may vote for your own photos in up to half the categories that go to a vote, rounded down. Four or five voting categories allow two self-votes.
  6. Report concerns. During category voting, use Report photo and give a short reason of up to 500 characters for a possible safety breach. Reporting does not cast a vote. Reports go privately to the host, who must review them before revealing category winners and can remove photos, warn or block players, or dismiss reports.
  7. Category winners. The highest-voted photos win each category. Tied photos share the category win, and each winning player receives a category win. The host reveals these results first.
  8. Overall winner. The player with the most category wins leads overall. The host selects Reveal Overall Winner separately. For a sole leading player, their highest-voted category-winning photo is selected. If several of that player's winning photos tie for highest votes, players vote to choose between those photos.
  9. Best photo of the game: tied players and repeat tie-breaks. If players tie for the most category wins, everyone votes among those players' category-winning photos. Each player has one vote per tie-break round; the category self-vote limit does not apply to these rounds. If the ballot ties, another round includes only the photos tied for the highest votes in the preceding round. This repeats until one photo wins, identifying the overall winner. The result then appears automatically.
  10. Close the game. After viewing the overall result, the host selects Close Game & Delete Data to delete all stored game photos, including winning photos, and remove the active game data. Leaving as a player does not close the game for everyone. After 24 hours without a game action, the game expires and automatic cleanup deletes all its stored photos and game data on the next scheduled run (every 15 minutes). Failed deletions are retried. Background polling does not reset the timer.
