Privacy Policy

Find It! by DesK Apps · Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains how Find It!, operated under the DesK Apps name, is intended to handle personal information. Please read it before playing. This policy must be checked against the live deployment and service configuration before public release.

1. Information used

When you join a game, the service may process your chosen player name, game code, photos you submit, votes, game progress, timestamps, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service. Avoid entering your real name or photographing personal information.

2. Why we use it

We use game information to create and join sessions, display submissions, conduct anonymous voting, calculate winners, support reconnection, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors. Our intended lawful basis for essential game processing is performance of the service requested by players; security processing may rely on legitimate interests, subject to assessment.

3. Photos and other players

Submitted photos are shown to other participants in the same game. Voting is designed to be anonymous, but a photograph may reveal who took it or identify people, locations or possessions. Do not upload images of people without appropriate permission, especially children, or anything private or sensitive.

4. Storage and deletion

The game is designed to remove temporary game data and non-winning photos when the game is ended, while retaining the overall winning image for display. Actual deletion timing, backups, logs and any winning-photo retention must be verified against the deployed Supabase configuration before launch. Do not rely on immediate irreversible deletion.

5. Service providers

Find It! uses Supabase for backend services and may be hosted through Netlify. These providers may process information on our behalf, including technical logs. Their processing locations and applicable safeguards should be confirmed before launch.

6. Advertising and purchases

Find It! plans to display advertising in waiting areas and offer optional challenge packs. If advertising or payment providers are enabled, this policy will be updated to identify them, explain their data use and any consent choices before those features launch. Test pack selection does not itself mean a payment has been taken.

7. Your choices and rights

UK users may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, object to certain processing and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Contact us with the game code and relevant details; please do not send sensitive information unnecessarily.

8. Children

Players should use Find It! with appropriate adult supervision where necessary. Age eligibility and parental-consent requirements must be finalised before public release.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email desk.apps.dk@gmail.com. The legal operator's full identity and postal contact details must be added before public release.

10. Changes

We may revise this policy when the game, hosting, advertising or payment arrangements change. The latest version will appear here.
